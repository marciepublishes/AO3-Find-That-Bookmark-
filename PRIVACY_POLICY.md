# Privacy Policy for AO3 Find That Bookmark!

Last updated: June 28, 2026

AO3 Find That Bookmark! is a browser extension that helps users identify deleted works from their own Archive of Our Own bookmarks. The extension operates locally and does not use developer-operated servers.

## Information Handled

The extension may process and store:

- Your AO3 username.
- Metadata visible on your AO3 bookmarks pages, including work titles, authors, fandoms, ratings, warnings, categories, relationships, characters, tags, word counts, chapter counts, summaries, and bookmark dates.
- Your bookmark notes and bookmark tags, including private bookmark information that AO3 displays to you while you are logged in.
- Local status information used to detect deleted works, such as check timestamps and deletion status.
- Extension settings.

The extension uses your browser's existing AO3 session when requesting your own bookmark pages. It does not read, store, or transmit your AO3 password or authentication-cookie value.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the extension's bookmark tracking, deletion detection, notification, backup, and restore features.

## Storage and Retention

Bookmark metadata and settings are stored locally in the extension's IndexedDB database on your device. Data remains until you clear the extension's data or uninstall the extension.

## Sharing and Transmission

The extension does not transmit stored bookmark metadata to the developer or to developer-operated servers. It does not sell data, display advertising, perform analytics, or share data for marketing.

The extension sends requests to `archiveofourown.org` as needed to import your bookmarks and check whether bookmarked works remain available. If you choose a Wayback Machine search link, your browser opens `web.archive.org` with the AO3 work identifier in the URL. The extension does not automatically fetch content from the Wayback Machine.

## Security

Network requests initiated by the extension use HTTPS. Local browser extension storage is protected by the security controls provided by your browser and operating system, but it is not represented as end-to-end encrypted storage.

## Limited Use

The extension's use of information is limited to providing and improving its single user-facing bookmark tracking and deleted-work identification purpose. Information is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes, and is not made available for humans to read except when a user voluntarily includes specific information in a support request.

The use of information received through browser and website access adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when extension functionality or legal requirements change. The latest version should be made available at the privacy policy URL listed in the Chrome Web Store.
