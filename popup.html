Update

The Oct 29 AO3 outage

While AO3 was down for maintenance on Oct 29, 2026, older versions of this extension mistook the outage for deletions. If you got a pile of "Deleted Work Detected" notifications, those were false alarms, and it's safe to dismiss them.

You don't need to do anything. Once AO3 is back up, the extension cleans this up on its own:

  • Works flagged on or after Oct 29 are quietly re-checked and come off your list if they're still up.
  • Deletions found before Oct 29 are kept exactly as they were.
  • Anything that really was deleted during the outage gets confirmed again within a day or two, without another notification.

It's also smarter now. When it sees AO3's maintenance page or server errors, it pauses instead of flagging anything.

How "Find That Bookmark!" works

Find That Bookmark keeps a small, local record of the works you've bookmarked on AO3. It saves just the details AO3 already shows on your bookmarks page, such as title, author, tags, and summary.

Every so often it checks whether those works are still up. If one has been deleted, it flags it, so you can finally answer "wait, which one was that?".

This extension is throttled on purpose.

Checking for deleted bookmarks happens every so often and at random intervals within a range of time, which is a low intensity way to interact with AO3's servers. The technical trade off is that a freshly deleted work won't be reported the instant it disappears. It also waits to confirm a work is without a doubt gone, rather than missing for a moment, before flagging it. In practice, a deletion is identified on the extension within a day or two rather than instantaneously.

In terms of privacy, all information is yours!

Nothing is uploaded, shared, or sent anywhere. There are no servers, and it can't bring deleted works back (only AO3 has those). It just tells you which of yours have gone missing.
